# Precon buy list — September 2026

What to buy for the three precons, by price tier. Source: EDHREC's upgrade
guide for each precon (how often a card is *added* to it) and the commander's
page (how often the commander's decks run it at all), minus everything already
in your collection, priced at Scryfall's cheapest printing in USD. Cardmarket
in EUR usually lands 10–20% under these numbers for commons and rares; the
expensive singles track closer.

The same list regenerates live from the tool — the **worth buying** section of
`python main.py <collection.csv> --precons`, or the *Worth buying* panel under
a precon in the web UI — so re-run it after a purchase and it drops what you
now own. Packages below are my grouping of that data into sensible orders;
the percentages are EDHREC's (added to X% of upgraded lists · run in Y% of the
commander's decks).

Packages are cumulative: Core assumes Budget, Splurge assumes both.
Everything here keeps the decks in Bracket 2 unless marked **GC** (Game
Changer, which moves a deck to bracket 3).

## If you buy one card per deck

- **Cloud:** Sram, Senior Edificer ($1.50) — or Buster Sword if the budget is there.
- **Terra:** Karmic Guide ($0.33).
- **Turtles:** Michelangelo, Weirdness to 11 ($2.39).

Swords to Plowshares and Path to Exile appear on all three lists; one copy of
each, split between the decks, covers the two that need removal most (Cloud
and the Turtles — Terra already has Mortify, Bedevil and Crackling Doom).
